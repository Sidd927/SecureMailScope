7.Detailed findings

Mail session carried no TLS — The mail session carried no TLS protection.

No TLS records were observed in this stream, so message and command content travelled in cleartext. This states what was observed. It does not attribute a cause: a session may be unprotected through client choice, server configuration, or interference, and this capture alone cannot distinguish those.

Recurrence counts the sessions in which a condition was observed. Findings are grouped by condition, so one misconfiguration seen many times is one issue with a recurrence count, not many issues.
