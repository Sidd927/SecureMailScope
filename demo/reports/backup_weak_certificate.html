7.Detailed findings

Certificate public key strength — A certificate uses an RSA key below the permitted length.

leaf uses a 1024-bit RSA key. NIST SP 800-57 Part 1 Rev.5 SS5.6.1: RSA moduli below 2048 bits provide less than 112 bits of security and are disallowed.

Certificate signature algorithm — A certificate in the chain is signed with a deprecated hash.

Signature algorithms observed: sha1WithRSAEncryption. SHA-1 -- RFC 9155 and NIST SP 800-131A Rev.2: SHA-1 must not be used for digital signature generation. A chain is only as sound as its weakest signature.

Recurrence counts the sessions in which a condition was observed. Findings are grouped by condition, so one misconfiguration seen many times is one issue with a recurrence count, not many issues.
